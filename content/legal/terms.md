## Payment terms

- Advance deposit: payment of 25% to 50% at the time of booking to secure reservations.
- Final balance: the remainder is due 15 to 30 days before the tour departure date.
- No EMI options: all bookings must be paid by standard full payment. No EMI or instalment payment plans are available.
- Last-minute bookings: 100% upfront payment is required if booked within 15 days of travel.
- Payment channels: bank transfers, UPI, credit/debit cards, or digital payment gateways.

## General agreement

- Agency scope: the company acts as an organiser and intermediary for transport, lodging and guided services.
- Legal compliance and customer liability: in case of any offence against the law, under Indian law or the law of the country the customer travels to, it is the customer's sole responsibility. The company bears no liability for any illegal acts, arrests or penalties faced by the traveller.
- Force majeure: the company may alter itineraries because of weather, strikes or disasters, without liability.
- Insurance and health: travellers must buy travel insurance and declare any pre-existing medical conditions.
- Legal jurisdiction: all disputes are handled strictly under Indian law, in the local city courts.
