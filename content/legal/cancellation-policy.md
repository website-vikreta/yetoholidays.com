## Timeline and deductions

- Over 30 days' notice: a small service fee is deducted.
- 15 to 30 days' notice: a 50% deduction applies.
- Less than 15 days' notice, or final and last-minute cancellation: the cancellation charge is 90% of the total tour cost.

## Third-party refund limits

Refunds are subject to hotel and airline refund caps, minus agency service fees.

## Refund speed

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 14 business days to the original account.
